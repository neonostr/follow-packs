# Follow Packs

**Discover your people on Nostr.**

Follow Packs makes it effortless to find and follow the most interesting voices on Nostr. Browse curated lists built by the community, or create your own to help others discover the people you love.

---

## What is it?

Finding the right people to follow on a new social protocol can be overwhelming. Follow Packs solves that. Think of it as curated starter kits — hand-picked collections of Nostr users organized by topic, interest, or vibe.

Whether you're brand new to Nostr or a seasoned user looking to expand your network, Follow Packs gets you connected to the conversations that matter to you.

## How it works

1. **Browse** — Explore packs created by the community, from developers and designers to artists and activists.
2. **Follow** — Found a pack you like? Follow everyone in it with a single click.
3. **Create** — Build your own pack and share it with the world. Highlight the voices you think deserve more attention.

## Why Follow Packs?

- 🚀 **Instant onboarding** — New to Nostr? Go from zero to a full, vibrant feed in seconds.
- 🎯 **Curated discovery** — Skip the noise. Follow packs made by people who know their niche.
- 🤝 **Community-powered** — Every pack is created by real Nostr users, not algorithms.
- 🔓 **Fully open** — Built on the Nostr protocol. No walled gardens, no gatekeepers.
