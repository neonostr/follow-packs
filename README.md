# Follow Packs

Find your people on Nostr. No algorithms, no nonsense.

---

Nostr is amazing, but let's be honest — starting from scratch with an empty feed sucks. You don't know who to follow, and scrolling through random notes hoping to stumble on someone interesting isn't exactly a great time.

Follow Packs fixes that.

People create packs — simple lists of Nostr users worth following. Maybe it's the best developers, the funniest shitposters, photographers, Bitcoin plebs, whatever. You find a pack you like, hit follow, and suddenly your feed is alive.

## The idea is simple

- **Browse packs** that other people have put together
- **Follow everyone** in a pack with one click
- **Make your own** and share the people you think deserve more eyes

That's it. No sign-up walls, no tracking, no corporate weirdness. It runs on Nostr, so everything is open and yours.
